# Use of AI (SI) tools in SEDNA

SEDNA was built during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 with the help of AI coding assistants. This file states where and how, as the Project Submission Guide requires.

## Tools

- **Claude Code** (Anthropic)
- **Codex** (OpenAI)

## How they were used

- **Code:** most source files in this repository were written or edited with AI assistance, under the team's direction and review. Every such file says so in its header comment.
- **Research support:** summarising public documentation of the SPHEREx archive, of the challenge, and of related tools, and analysing whether the team's idea of a distance focus was feasible with SPHEREx data. The idea of focusing the sky by distance came from the team; the AI assessed its feasibility, and the decision tests in `docs/decision-tests.md` checked it on real data.
- **Tests and checks:** writing probe scripts and tests, and reading their results.
- **Demo video:** the 30-second video (`docs/video/sedna-demo-30s.mp4`) is a recording of the published site, made by a script written with Claude Code's help (`web/scripts/record-demo.mjs`): it drives the real app scene by scene, shows each narration line as an English subtitle, and times each scene to its line. **The narration voice is synthetic:** Microsoft's neural text-to-speech (voice en-US-EmmaNeural, through the open-source `edge-tts` package, `web/scripts/make-narration.py`) reads the team's script (`web/scripts/narration.json`). A label on every frame says so: "Made with AI assistance: logo, animations, and this synthetic narration voice". The video has no AI-generated images and no music; every picture in it is the real site showing real SPHEREx data.
- **Visual identity and documentation:** the team designed the identity: the name, the historical research behind it, the references, the initial sketches, the style guide, the colours and the typography. From that direction, Codex generated the final assets: the SEDNA logo variants and vector artwork, the brand guide and gallery, the SVG animations and their video exports in the [motion kit](docs/brand/motion/), and the English translation of the kit. As the Space Apps rules ask for AI-assisted images and videos, the kit's presentation images and motion videos carry a visible "Made with AI assistance" label (and the same note in their metadata, `web/scripts/label-ai-assets.py`), and the README says so under the logo. The logo files themselves are kept clean so they can be used. The kit is in [`docs/brand`](docs/brand/).

## What the team did

- Chose the challenge, the product and its scope, and decided what to build and what to cut.
- Ran the decision tests on real SPHEREx data and judged their results.
- Reviewed, tested and integrated every change.

## What AI did not produce

Every astronomical image, spectrum, light curve and measurement shown by SEDNA comes from processing real SPHEREx data, with Gaia and JPL Horizons used as references. No scientific content in the tool is generated by AI. The AI-assisted visual assets described above are brand artwork. The motion videos are silent renders of the brand animations.
